# Baby Claw

Use `baby_claw_healthcheck` to verify that the Baby Claw OpenClaw plugin is loaded and responding.

This Step 1 scaffold does not create, read, or transmit baby care records. Do not ask for or expose private keys, encryption keys, image data, raw baby records, or unencrypted notes.
